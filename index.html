Privacy Policy

App Name: Multi Notification Saver

Developer: Md Ismail

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Multi Notification Saver operates 100% locally on your device. Your captured notification history is stored securely on your phone using an encrypted local database and is NEVER transmitted, uploaded, or shared with any external server.

1. Information Capture & Local Storage

The App uses Android's Notification Access service to save incoming notifications from messaging apps (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, IMO, Viber, Discord, Instagram, etc.).

  • Local Storage: Notification message text, sender name, app icon, and timestamp are saved exclusively in your phone's internal SQLite Room database.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not operate any backend servers. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free, we integrate Google AdMob to display Banner and Interstitial Ads.

  • Data Processed by AdMob: Google AdMob may process device identifiers (Google Advertising ID), IP address, and interaction data for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • AdMob Policy: AdMob complies with global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA). Learn more at Google Advertising Policy.

3. Required Android Permissions

  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE: Required to read incoming notifications.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ for status alerts.
  • INTERNET: Required by Google AdMob to load ads.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You can delete any notification message inside the App anytime. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all saved notification history from your device.
